# Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Strategy

## Overview
I designed a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) strategy for SecureHealth to strengthen defenses against ransomware and healthcare‑specific cyber threats. The strategy enhances detection, incident response, and compliance with HIPAA regulations.

## Objectives
- Improve detection of ransomware and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
- Protect patient data and ensure HIPAA compliance
- Strengthen incident response and recovery capabilities
- Increase visibility into emerging threats and adversary tactics

## Scope
- Departments: IT Security, Incident Response, Compliance, Network Administration, Executive Management
- Systems: EMR platforms, telehealth, cloud apps, endpoints, servers, vendor access, backup systems
- Boundaries: Internal networks, remote access, vendor connections, healthcare operations

## Threat Assessment
- **High Priority:** Ransomware, credential theft, phishing
- **Medium Priority:** Third‑party vendor risks, data breaches
- Key vulnerabilities: weak passwords, unpatched systems, excessive privileges, phishing exposure

## Intelligence Sources
- **Internal:** SIEM logs, endpoint telemetry, authentication logs, incident reports
- **External:** Recorded Future, Healthcare ISAC, OSINT, government advisories, dark web monitoring
- Tools: CrowdStrike EDR, SIEM dashboards, threat intelligence feeds

## Processes
- Automated IOC ingestion and correlation
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping and behavioral analysis
- Dissemination protocols: real‑time SOC alerts, daily IR reports, monthly executive summaries

## Capability Building
- Tools: Recorded Future, CrowdStrike, Veeam, SIEM, MFA solutions
- Team roles: CTI analysts, SOC analysts, incident responders, compliance officers, threat hunters
- Training: ransomware analysis, threat hunting, HIPAA awareness, phishing detection

## Collaboration
- Internal: dashboards, email alerts, weekly meetings, executive briefings
- External: H‑ISAC, CISA, FBI, vendors, industry communities
- Benefits: shared intelligence, faster detection, improved coordination

## Effectiveness Measurement
- KPIs: MTTD, MTTR, ransomware prevention rate, phishing click rate, IOC detection, compliance audit results
- Continuous improvement: quarterly reviews, updated playbooks, expanded threat coverage

## Conclusion
This CTI strategy strengthens SecureHealth’s resilience against ransomware and healthcare‑focused threats. By integrating intelligence sources, aligning with HIPAA, and building collaborative processes, the organization improves detection, response, and long‑term cybersecurity posture.
